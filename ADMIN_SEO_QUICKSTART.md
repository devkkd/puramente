# Admin SEO Quick Start Guide

## How to Edit Product SEO

### Step 1: Navigate to Product Edit Page
1. Go to Admin Dashboard
2. Click **Products** in the sidebar
3. Find the product you want to edit
4. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon)

### Step 2: Scroll to SEO Section
- The product edit page has multiple sections
- Scroll down past "Media & Status" section
- You'll find the **"SEO Optimization"** section at the bottom

### Step 3: Fill in SEO Fields

#### Meta Title (Page Title)
- **What it is**: The title that appears in Google search results and browser tabs
- **Max length**: 60 characters
- **Example**: "Premium Gold Necklace with Gemstones | Puramente"
- **Tips**:
  - Include your main product keyword
  - Add "| Puramente" at the end for brand recognition
  - Make it descriptive but concise

#### Meta Description (Search Result Snippet)
- **What it is**: The preview text shown under your title in search results
- **Max length**: 160 characters
- **Example**: "Handcrafted gold necklace with premium gemstones. Luxury jewelry for special occasions. Shop our exclusive collection online."
- **Tips**:
  - Include main keyword naturally
  - Add a call-to-action (Shop, Explore, Discover, etc.)
  - Write for both humans AND search engines
  - Make it unique for each product

#### Meta Keywords
- **What it is**: Words that describe your product (used for search relevance)
- **Max count**: 10 keywords
- **Example keywords**:
  - "gold necklace"
  - "luxury jewelry"
  - "gemstone necklace"
  - "handcrafted jewelry"
  - "fine jewels"
- **Tips**:
  - Focus on 5-8 highly relevant phrases
  - Use 2-4 word phrases, not just single words
  - Think about how customers search for this product
  - Include product type, material, and feature words

### Step 4: Preview Your SEO
- Under each field, you'll see how it appears in search results
- **Title preview**: Shows as blue underlined link
- **Description preview**: Shows how it appears in search results

### Step 5: Save
- Click the **"Save SEO Settings"** button (blue button at bottom of SEO section)
- You'll see a success message: "SEO settings saved successfully!"

## How to Edit Category SEO

### Step 1: Navigate to Category Edit Page
1. Go to Admin Dashboard
2. Click **Categories** in the sidebar
3. Find the category you want to edit
4. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon)

### Step 2: Fill in Category Details
- **Category Name**: e.g., "Necklaces" (used internally)
- **Home Name**: e.g., "Graceful Necklaces" (displayed on homepage)
- Upload/update images if needed

### Step 3: Scroll to SEO Section
- Same as products - scroll to the bottom
- Find the **"SEO Optimization"** section

### Step 4: Fill in SEO Fields
- Follow the same guidelines as products
- **Example for category**:
  - Title: "Gold Necklaces Collection | Luxury Jewelry | Puramente"
  - Description: "Browse our premium collection of handcrafted gold necklaces. Available with or without gemstones. Luxury jewelry for everyday elegance."
  - Keywords: "necklace collection", "gold jewelry", "luxury necklaces", "gemstone jewelry"

### Step 5: Save
- Click **"Save SEO Settings"** button
- Confirm success message appears

## SEO Tips & Best Practices

### ✅ DO's

✅ **Include Your Main Keyword**
- Make sure each product/category has its primary keyword in title and description
- Example: If selling "Emerald Rings", include "Emerald Ring" in title

✅ **Write for Humans First**
- Read your meta description out loud
- Does it sound natural? Great!
- Does it sound like a robot wrote it? Rewrite it!

✅ **Make Each One Unique**
- Don't copy-paste SEO data between products
- Each product/category is unique and deserves unique descriptions

✅ **Use All 60 Characters for Title**
- Longer titles are better (50-60 chars) to maximize visibility
- Don't waste space with short titles

✅ **Include Call-to-Action**
- "Shop Now", "Explore", "Discover", "Browse", "View"
- Makes users more likely to click

✅ **Update When Things Change**
- Update SEO when you change product details
- Seasonal updates: "Best Summer Jewelry", "Holiday Gifts"

### ❌ DON'Ts

❌ **Keyword Stuffing**
- Don't repeat keywords multiple times
- "Gold necklace gold jewelry gold pendant" = BAD ❌
- "Premium gold necklace jewelry" = GOOD ✅

❌ **Misleading Information**
- Don't put promises you can't keep
- "Free Shipping" in title = actually need to offer free shipping

❌ **Using Special Characters**
- Avoid: | ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) [ ] { }
- These can break search display
- Stick to: Letters, numbers, hyphens, apostrophes

❌ **Copying from Competitors**
- Write your own unique descriptions
- Google penalizes duplicate content

❌ **Forgetting to Save**
- Always click "Save SEO Settings"
- Changes don't take effect until saved!

## Checking Your Work

### Before You Save:

1. **Read the title out loud**
   - Does it sound natural? ✅
   - Is it descriptive? ✅
   - Does it include your keyword? ✅

2. **Read the description out loud**
   - Does it flow smoothly? ✅
   - Does it tempt someone to click? ✅
   - Is there a clear benefit or CTA? ✅

3. **Check the character counters**
   - Title: 50-60 characters ✅
   - Description: 150-160 characters ✅
   - Keywords: 5-10 relevant phrases ✅

4. **Review the preview**
   - Click on the preview links
   - Imagine seeing this in Google search
   - Would YOU click on it? 🤔

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Save SEO Settings" button is greyed out
**Solution**: 
- Click inside one of the fields first
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled in your browser
- Try refreshing the page

### Problem: Characters keep disappearing when I type
**Solution**:
- This shouldn't happen! Report to your developer
- For now: Write in a text editor, then copy/paste

### Problem: Keywords won't add
**Solution**:
- Press **Enter** after typing the keyword
- OR click the **Add** button
- Maximum 10 keywords allowed

### Problem: "SEO settings saved successfully" but changes didn't appear
**Solution**:
- Wait 5 seconds (cache updating)
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5 on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac)
- The data is saved even if display is slow
